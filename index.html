I am Mohammad Rafiq Siddiqi, a digital marketing and e-commerce learner from Afghanistan, working to turn foundational knowledge into real-world experience.

I am actively using the core tools, platforms, and workflows of digital marketing—from social media management and Meta advertising to content planning, audience targeting, market research, and campaign analysis. I learn by doing, testing, observing results, and improving my approach.

I completed a six-month Digital Marketing & E-Commerce training program under UNICEF’s Skills and Livelihood Program, implemented by NETLINKS Ltd. I am also completing the final year of high school, while developing my professional skills and English communication.

My goal is to grow into a capable digital marketing professional who can help businesses communicate clearly, reach relevant audiences, and make better-informed marketing decisions.

Notes & learning

Marketing insights

Short articles and practical notes—an evolving space for ideas, frameworks, and lessons from my learning journey.

PAID MEDIA · NOTE 01

Why campaign objectives matter

The campaign objective influences delivery, optimization, and the actions an advertising platform prioritizes. Choosing it should reflect the business goal.

ANALYTICS · NOTE 02

Learning to read campaign metrics

Metrics become useful when interpreted in context. Reach, cost, and conversations tell different parts of the story and should be evaluated together.

CONTENT · NOTE 03

Consistency before complexity

Simple planning systems, clear messaging, and consistent publishing can create a practical foundation for a small business’s online presence.
